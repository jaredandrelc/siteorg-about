Join our Community

The UST SITE (Society of Information Technology Enthusiasts) is the official academic and parent student organization for the Information Technology department within the University of Santo Tomas. Associated with the College of Information and Computing Sciences (CICS), it fosters student development, technology advocacy, and industry connections.
