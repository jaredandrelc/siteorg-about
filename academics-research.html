Meet the creators

Get to know the dedicated Information Technology students from the College of Information and Computing Sciences behind the SITE ecosystem. We are a passionate team of designers and developers committed to enhancing the student experience. Whether you are exploring our mission on About SITE, seeking support via Frontdesk, testing your knowledge with Quizmix, or collaborating with peers on Studypal, our goal is to build innovative tools that empower our academic community.
